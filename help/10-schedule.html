Your Schedule

Put your season on Lockerbook ahead of time. On game day, tap the game, set the lineup, and start scoring.

Add games one at a time

Team → Add to Schedule → Add Game to Schedule. Enter the opponent, date and time (or turn on Time to be announced), Home/Away, and the field. You set the lineup on game day.

Import your whole schedule

Team → Add to Schedule → Import Schedule…

From a calendar link (best — you can refresh it later)

  1. On your school's athletics site or league site, find Subscribe, Add to Calendar, or iCal and copy the link. It usually starts with webcal:// or https://.
  2. Paste it into Lockerbook and tap Load Calendar.
  3. School calendars often include every sport. Use the filter box (it starts with your sport, like "Soccer") to keep only your games. Try "Girls Varsity" if your school lists several teams.
  4. Check the games you want and tap Import.

From a file

  • A calendar file (.ics), or
  • A spreadsheet saved as CSV with columns like:
Date Time Opponent H/A Location
9/18/2026 4:00 PM Rival High A Rival Field
9/22/2026 TBA Hill Academy H

No H/A column? Write the opponent as "vs Hill Academy" (home) or "@ Rival High" (away).

Games that are already on your schedule show Already on your schedule and aren't added twice.

Keep it up to date

If you imported from a link, use Add to Schedule → Refresh from Calendar Link. Games that moved get their new date, time, and field, and new games can be added. Games you've already started are never changed.

Edit or delete a scheduled game

Swipe left on the game → Edit or Delete. Delete asks first.

Games that went by without a score

A scheduled game whose date has passed moves to Past, not played, folded away under your upcoming games. Tap one to score it late, or swipe left to edit or delete it.

Folding the lists away

A full season is a long list, and it sits above your roster. Tap the Upcoming or Games heading to fold that list; the number beside the heading tells you how many are in there. Folded, Upcoming still shows your next game, so game day is still one tap. Lockerbook remembers how you left it.

Privacy

Loading a calendar link only downloads that calendar. Lockerbook doesn't send anything about your team.
