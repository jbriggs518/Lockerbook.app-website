Live View for Families and Fans

Coming soon: live viewing over the internet arrives in a future beta. You can choose your settings now and they'll apply automatically.

Who can watch

Audience What they see
Coaching staff Everything
Invited viewers (family you invite) Score, clock, goals and assists, cards, and player names — unless you turn on Hide names
Public link Score, clock, goals and cards with jersey numbers only. Never names, substitutions, or notes

Invited and public viewing are off until you turn them on for a game. For club and youth teams, Hide names from invited viewers starts on.

Change settings

Game → ••• → Live View Sharing. Use Preview as to see exactly what each audience sees.

Streaming your game on the NFHS Network? See Streaming and NFHS Network to add a Watch link and the on-screen scoreboard overlay.
