Scoring a Soccer Game

Other sports use the stat sheet. See Scoring other sports.

Before kickoff

From your schedule (see Your schedule):

  1. Open your team and tap the game under Upcoming.
  2. Starting lineup: tap each starter. Swipe right on your goalkeeper and tap Set GK.
  3. Check the format and Live view options (see Live view), then tap Ready.

A game that isn't on your schedule:

  1. Open your team → Add Games → Start a Game Now.
  2. Enter the opponent, date/time, Home/Away/Neutral, and (optionally) the field.
  3. Pick the lineup and live view options, then tap Ready.

You can change the lineup until the first half starts. Tap Edit Lineup on the game screen.

The scoring screen

  • Scoreboard at the top: score, period, and clock. Tap it to adjust the clock.
  • Start 1st Half starts the clock. Use Pause/Resume for stoppages if your clock stops, and End 1st Half at the whistle.
  • Two button pads: your team and the opponent.

Your team's buttons

Button What happens
Goal Tap the scorer, then the assist (or No assist). Turn on Penalty kick first if it was a PK
Shot Saved Shot on goal that their keeper saved. Pick the shooter
Shot Off Target / Blocked / Post-Bar Missed shots. Pick the shooter, or Player not sure
Corner Recorded instantly, or pick who took it if Track Corner Kick Takers is on
Steal Pick the player who won the ball (or Player not sure)
Foul / Offside Pick the player (or not sure)
Yellow / Red Pick the player (bench players included). A red card removes the player from the field
Sub Tap the player coming off, then the player going on
Change GK Pick the new keeper from players on the field
Own Goal (theirs) The opponent put it in their own net — counts for you

Opponent buttons

One tap each: Goal, Save by GK (your keeper saved it), Shot Off Target, Shot Blocked, Corner, Foul, Offside, Yellow, Red, and Own Goal (ours) (pick your player if you know who).

Tracking the other team's players (optional)

Turn on Track Opposing Team Stats in your team's settings (open your team → Edit Team → Scoring).

When it's on:

  • Goal asks Who scored? Type the jersey number and tap Next, then type the assist number and tap Save. Tap No assist if there wasn't one, or Number not sure if you didn't see it. The minute comes from the game clock.
  • Save by GK, Shot Off Target, Shot Blocked, Foul, Offside, Yellow, and Red ask for one number (or Number not sure).
  • The play-by-play shows the numbers, like GOAL – Sample Academy #10, assist #7.

Opponent roster (optional). To score them exactly like your own team, add their roster before the game: in game setup tap roster, or on the game screen tap ••• → Roster. Add jersey numbers (names are optional), Paste cells copied from a spreadsheet, or Copy from last game against the same team. Their numbers then appear as buttons. Any number you type during the game that isn't on the list is added for you.

Opponent names stay on your device for your staff. Families, the public link, and the broadcast overlay only ever see the other team's jersey numbers, and exports never include opponent names.

Fixing mistakes

  • Undo (top right) removes the last thing you entered — including a period start or end.
  • Long-press any line in the play-by-play → Delete.
  • After the game, the head coach can tap Edit Stats to correct anything (see Fixing stats after the game). Reopen is for games that aren't really over, and it also needs the head coach PIN.

Clock tips

  • The clock keeps running if you lock the phone or switch apps.
  • Adjust Clock (tap the scoreboard or ••• menu) nudges the time by 1, 10, or 60 seconds to match the official clock. On count-down clocks, + means more time has passed.
  • +1:30 on a count-down clock means 1:30 of stoppage time.

Ending the game

  • End the last period. If the score is tied and your format has overtime, Start OT1 appears.
  • Tap Final and confirm.
