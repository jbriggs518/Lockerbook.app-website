Box Score and Stats

Open a game → ••• → Box Score. It updates live during the game.

Players

Column Meaning
MIN Minutes on the field (from your lineup and subs)
G / A Goals / assists
SH All shots
SOG Shots on goal — goals plus saved shots
PK Penalty kicks scored / taken (during play)
FC Fouls committed
YC / RC Yellow / red cards
(s) Came on as a substitute

Goalkeepers

Column Meaning
MIN Minutes in goal
SOG Shots on goal faced
SV Saves
GA Goals against (own goals included)
SV% Saves ÷ shots on goal faced
DEC Win/Loss/Tie — goes to the keeper with the most minutes
SO Shutout — no goals allowed and only one keeper used

Opponent players

Shows up when you track the other team's players (see Tracking the other team's players). One row per jersey number: G, A, SH, SOG, PK, FC, OFF (offsides), YC, RC. Names show only if you entered their roster. Plays entered as Number not sure still count in the team totals.

Team

Side-by-side shots, shots on goal, saves, corners, fouls, offsides, and cards.
