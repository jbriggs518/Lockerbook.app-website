Lockerbook — User Manual

Lockerbook is the scorebook for every sport and every level: school, college, and club/travel. Keep the book fast, get a full box score, and send stats to MaxPreps, while your players' information stays with your staff.

Beta note: this manual describes Lockerbook 0.1 (TestFlight). Features marked Coming soon aren't in your build yet. Sports marked Preview use the stat-sheet scorekeeper.
