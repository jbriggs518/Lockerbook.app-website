Privacy and Security

Lockerbook was built so your players' information stays with your staff.

  • What we keep: player name, jersey number, and (optionally) position, graduation year, and MaxPreps athlete ID. Plus game stats and your notes.
  • What we never collect: phone numbers, emails, addresses, birthdays, photos, location, or health information.
  • Where it's stored: on your device, encrypted by iOS whenever the device is locked. When sync arrives, it will use your own iCloud account — Lockerbook doesn't keep a copy on its own servers.
  • No ads, no tracking, no selling data.
  • Lock the app: Settings → Require Face ID / passcode. Lockerbook also hides its screen in the app switcher.
  • Public viewers only ever see jersey numbers.
  • Delete data: delete a team from its screen, or everything from Settings → Delete All Lockerbook Data.

Questions: contact@lockerbook.app
