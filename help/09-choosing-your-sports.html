Choosing Your Sports

Lockerbook keeps the book for 15 sports. The first time you open it, pick the sports you keep the book for. Lockerbook remembers your choice and only shows those sports.

Change your sports

Settings → Your Sports → tap sports to turn them on or off → Save.

  • Turning a sport off hides it. Its teams and games stay on your device and come back when you turn the sport on again. Lockerbook tells you how many teams will be hidden before you save.
  • You need at least one sport turned on.
  • The team list shows a row like "2 teams hidden for sports you've turned off." Tap it to go straight to Your Sports.

Plans (coming soon)

Once subscriptions launch, you'll choose the sports in your plan: one sport, or all sports. Schools and clubs will be able to cover every team with one license.
