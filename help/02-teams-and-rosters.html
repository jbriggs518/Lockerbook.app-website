Teams and Rosters

Teams

Each team is one roster for one season — for example, "Example High Girls Varsity, 2026 Fall." Create separate teams for JV and varsity.

MaxPreps (high school teams in sports MaxPreps imports): the first time you open a team, Lockerbook asks Will this team post stats to MaxPreps?

  • Yes shows the MaxPreps stats file, upload steps, and MaxPreps reminders on the team, box score, and export screens.
  • No hides them. You can still share the box score sheet and spreadsheet.
  • Change the answer anytime in Edit Team → MaxPreps. College and club/travel teams don't see the question, because MaxPreps covers U.S. high schools.

Levels: High School (Varsity, JV, Freshman), College, and Club / Travel. Each has its own default game format for your sport.

Soccer scoring settings (open the team → Edit Team → Scoring):

  • Track Opposing Team Stats asks for the other team's jersey numbers on their goals, shots, fouls and cards.
  • Track Corner Kick Takers asks who took each of your corners, so corner kicks can go to MaxPreps.

Edit or delete: open the team and scroll to the bottom. Deleting a team removes its roster and every game on this device. This can't be undone.

Players

Field Required Notes
First / last name At least one Shown to staff, and to invited family viewers unless you hide names
Jersey number Yes Up to 3 digits; "00" is allowed. Must be unique among active players
Positions No Soccer: pick one or more; the first is primary, and goalkeepers are suggested as the starting keeper. Other sports: type it (for example P/SS, PG, QB, Libero, or a weight class)
Graduation year No
MaxPreps athlete ID No Helps MaxPreps match the right player on import
Active — Turn off for players who leave; their past stats stay

Lockerbook does not ask for or store phone numbers, emails, addresses, birthdays, or photos.

Lots of players? Import them from a spreadsheet. See Import and export your roster.

Game formats

Preset Halves/quarters Clock Overtime
High School (NFHS) 2 × 40 Counts down 2 × 10, sudden victory
High School JV 2 × 35 Counts down None
College – Regular Season 2 × 45 Counts up None
College – Postseason 2 × 45 Counts up 2 × 10, sudden victory
Club / Youth 2 × 35 Counts up None
Youth – Quarters 4 × 12 (7v7) Counts up None

Rules vary by state and league. Adjust the number of periods, minutes, clock direction, and players on the field to match yours.

Positions

Player → Positions → tap every position the player plays. The first one you pick is the primary position; tap Make Primary or drag (Edit) to change the order.
