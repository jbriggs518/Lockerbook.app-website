Short description

Lockerbook is the scorebook for every sport: fast on the sideline, detailed in the stats, private to the team, and ready for MaxPreps.

Long description

Lockerbook replaces the paper scorebook for coaches and scorekeepers across 15 sports. A scorekeeper records each play with one or two taps. Lockerbook builds the box score as the game goes, including minutes and goalkeeper decisions in soccer, at-bats and pitching lines in baseball and softball, and makes and misses in basketball. For high school teams, Lockerbook creates the stats file MaxPreps imports, carrying jersey numbers rather than names. Lockerbook keeps only what a scorebook needs (name, jersey number, position and graduation year), stores it on the device, and has no ad networks or tracking.

Key features

  • 15 sports: a complete soccer pad and a stat-sheet scorekeeper for 11 more
  • Game formats for high school, college, club and travel play
  • Box scores, a printable entry sheet and spreadsheet export
  • MaxPreps stats file for high school teams
  • Roster and schedule import, sample team, head-coach corrections with an audit log
  • Face ID lock, privacy screen, jersey-numbers-only public outputs
