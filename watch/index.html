Jersey numbers only.

This page shows the score, the clock and plays by jersey number. It never shows athlete names or contact information, for either team, and it never shows anything about an opposing athlete beyond a jersey number. There are no ads, analytics or trackers here, and no third-party scripts, fonts or images — the only thing this page remembers is the last code you typed, kept in your own browser.
