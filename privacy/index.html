Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 24, 2026

Lockerbook ("the app") is published by Vermonster's Pro Shop ("we," "us"). Lockerbook helps coaches and scorekeepers keep sports statistics. We built it so student-athlete information stays with the team.

The short version

  • Lockerbook keeps your team's information on your device. We don't run servers that hold it.
  • We don't collect personal information through the app, and we have no analytics, ads, or tracking.
  • If a coach turns on the live link for a game, the score, clock and scoring plays — with jersey numbers only, never names — are published through Apple's iCloud so family and friends can follow the game. It's off unless the coach turns it on.
  • Lockerbook never asks for athletes' contact information, birthdays, photos, health information, or location.

Information you enter

Coaches and scorekeepers can enter:

  • Team details: school or club name, mascot, level, and season
  • Players: first and last name, jersey number, and optionally position, graduation year, and a MaxPreps athlete identifier
  • Games: opponent, date, location, lineups, statistics, and staff notes
  • Optionally, the other team's jersey numbers and player names, for staff use only

This information is stored only on your device. It's protected by iOS Data Protection (encrypted while the device is locked), and you can turn on a Face ID or passcode lock in the app.

Information we collect

No personal information. The only thing the app sends to a place we can see is the live link's public scoreboard, described below, which contains no personal information. Lockerbook has no user accounts, analytics, advertising, or third-party SDKs, and it doesn't track you across apps or websites. We don't sell or share personal information.

If you choose to share crash reports or usage data with app developers in your device settings, Apple may give us aggregated, de-identified information about app performance.

If you email us, we use your message only to reply.

Network use

Lockerbook works offline. It connects to the internet only when you ask it to load a schedule from a calendar link you entered, when you tap a link (for example, a game's stream link), which opens in Safari or another app, or while a game's live link is on (below).

Sharing

Nothing leaves your device unless you share it:

  • Files you export, such as the MaxPreps stats file (jersey numbers and statistics only, with no names), the printable entry sheet, the box score spreadsheet, or a roster file. You choose where they go through the iOS share sheet.
  • The live link, only for a game where a coach turns it on. While it's on, the app publishes a public scoreboard for that game to Lockerbook's public database in Apple's iCloud (CloudKit): the team and opponent names as entered, the score, the clock and period, and the scoring plays written with jersey numbers only. It never includes an athlete's name, nickname, position, substitutions, injuries, notes or anything about the opposing players beyond jersey numbers. Names are removed on the coach's device before anything is sent. Anyone with the game's code can view it at lockerbook.app/watch without an account. The scoreboard is deleted when the coach turns the link off or replaces the code, and otherwise one week after the game. Apple stores it under its own privacy policy; we don't use it for anything but showing the game.
  • The lockerbook.app/watch page has no analytics, cookies or trackers. It remembers the last code typed on that device in the browser only.

Students and children

Lockerbook is meant to be used by coaches, staff, and other adults. Athlete information, including for athletes under 13, is entered by adults on behalf of their team and stays on their device; the live link carries jersey numbers only. We don't knowingly collect personal information from children. Schools and clubs that need a data privacy agreement can contact us.

Your choices

  • Edit or delete any player, game, or team in the app.
  • Delete everything with Settings → Delete All Lockerbook Data, or by deleting the app.
  • Don't want names on a device? Jersey numbers alone work for scoring.

Security

Lockerbook uses iOS Data Protection for its data file, an optional Face ID or passcode lock, a privacy screen in the app switcher, and a head-coach PIN (stored as a salted hash) for changing final stats. Exported files are created with file protection and removed from temporary storage when you leave the export screen.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and the date above. Before any new feature sends team information off your device, we'll update this policy first.
